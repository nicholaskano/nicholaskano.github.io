About

Behind the camera.

My name is Nicholas Kano, and I have been doing film and digital photography for five years, off and on. My heart has always leaned toward the physicality involved in film photography, especially being in the darkroom and developing photos. I love photography, fashion, music, and essentially all creative mediums, which continue to inspire me throughout my path in life. This portfolio offers a glimpse into the diversity of photos I have taken over the years, including both film and digital photography.
